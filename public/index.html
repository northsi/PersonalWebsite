About

I am a Ph.D. student in Physics at HKUST. My research focuses on unconventional superconductivity and electronic and thermal transport in quantum materials, using theoretical and computational methods. I received my B.Sc. in Physics from Chongqing University in 2025.

Research interests: condensed-matter theory, superconducting pairing, symmetry and group representation theory, and transport in topological and quantum materials.

Research Experience

Unconventional Superconductivity

2025 – 2026

HKUST

  • Developed symmetry-based classifications of superconducting pairing channels in multiorbital quantum systems using group representation theory.
  • Constructed effective Hamiltonians and Bogoliubov–de Gennes (BdG) models, and analyzed their symmetry and topological properties using analytical and numerical methods.
  • Worked with operator representations, basis transformations, low-energy projections, and symmetry constraints.

First-Principles Electronic and Thermal Transport

2023 – 2025

Chongqing University

  • Performed first-principles and Boltzmann-transport calculations of electronic and lattice thermal transport in topological and quantum materials.
  • Analyzed the interplay among electronic structure, electron–hole compensation, and thermal and electrical transport.
